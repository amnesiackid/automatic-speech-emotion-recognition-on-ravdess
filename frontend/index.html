About

Record a few seconds of speech or upload an audio file, and the page sends it to the local prediction server. Audio is classified by a DistilHuBERT model fine-tuned on the RAVDESS dataset. Built for the Computational Linguistics Team Laboratory (Phonetics) at the University of Stuttgart.
